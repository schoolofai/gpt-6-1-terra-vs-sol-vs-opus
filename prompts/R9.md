Look at the two reference photos in this folder (ref_front.jpg and ref_side.jpg). They show a traditional tower windmill.
Build a 3D model of this windmill in a single self-contained file called index.html using three.js (load it from a CDN such as https://unpkg.com/three@0.160.0 via an import map). Requirements:
- It must be real 3D (a WebGL scene with a perspective camera, lighting and shadows), not a 2D drawing.
- Match the references: dark green tapering octagonal tower, white domed cap, four lattice sails, the wooden balcony around the middle, red door, the tail pole at the back, grassy ground.
- Animate the mechanism: the sails turn on their axle continuously; add a visible inner mechanism too (for example the axle and a gear wheel visible through a cut-away or open hatch) that turns in sync.
- Slowly orbit the camera around the windmill automatically, and allow mouse orbit (OrbitControls).
- No console errors. Do not ask questions; finish in one go.
